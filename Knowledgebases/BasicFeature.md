### **How to Sell in CC:**
1. Obtain a **Surat Ijin Dagang** from the Server Shop / Admin Shop (CC LV).
2. Right-click on any container → **Convert to Store**.
3. Place the items you want to sell inside your shop container.
4. Right-click → **Edit Store** to modify the price, shop title, and shop description.
5. Right-click -> **Collect Earnings** to collect your shop profit.
6. Right-click → **Close Store** to shut down your store and collect any earnings left in that particualar store.
